# Reference Notes

**Status: Orientation references, not evidentiary proof.**

This file collects references that may help readers understand general background, conventional terminology, and neighboring fields related to TCP, Closure Phase Ψ, 神の領域原理, and public specification practice.

These references are **not** presented as evidence that TCP, Closure Phase Ψ, 神の領域原理, or HDS are true.  
They are not proof of the framework.  
They are not authority shields.

They are included only to show:

- common usage of specification language;
- adjacent engineering and conformance practices;
- general AI risk and explainability vocabulary;
- public-archive and licensing conventions used by this repository;
- background material for the Umami Gap analogy;
- general-language references that help explain why certain terms are separated.

The framework itself remains a public specification archive. The proper evaluation mode is **conformance**, not belief.
